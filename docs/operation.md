# Operation and maintenance overview

## Normal operation

Movement within a PIR sensor's coverage area activates the lights connected to that sensor's built-in relay. The lights remain active while movement continues and switch off after approximately 15 minutes of inactivity.

## Functional observation

1. Observe the lights turning on when someone enters a monitored area.
2. Leave that area without further movement in the sensor coverage.
3. Observe the automatic switch-off after approximately 15 minutes.
4. Repeat across the monitored floor areas to assess coverage.

The project covers approximately 8 sensor units and 16 lights; the repository does not assign a fixed number of lights to every individual sensor.

## Maintenance

Keep sensor views unobstructed and report lighting faults or unexpected switching to the campus electrical maintenance team. If space usage or partitions change, review sensor coverage and timing with that team.

The photographs and Tinkercad diagrams document the project. Electrical modifications to the building installation require qualified campus electrical personnel and the actual sensor manufacturer's instructions.
